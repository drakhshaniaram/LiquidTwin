# 02 Business Problem

## Context
Terminals move multiple products through shared tank headers, common headers, pumps and valves. Lineups are typically planned manually in spreadsheets or on SCADA mimic screens.

## Pain points
| Problem | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Shared common headers and jetty lines | Conflicts between jobs; demurrage |
| Multi-product pipes | Flushing and changeover cost (time, volume, off-spec risk) |
| Maintenance/cleaning windows change daily | Plans invalidated late |
| Underground vs aboveground, slope, pump head | Real achievable rates differ from nominal |
| ETA/ETC from work orders vs. tank stock/ullage | Late vessels, blocked tanks |
| No what-if capability | Planner relies on experience |

## Value
- Lower changeover/flush volume and valve operations.
- Fewer late jobs (lateness penalty minimized).
- Faster planning, with explanations.
- A shared visual language for planners, operators and management.

## KPIs
Total objective, number of late jobs, total lateness (min), flush volume (m3), valve operations, common-header usage, plan time (s), jetty/line utilization.
